## Kannu 2.0.0 — Horus

Kannu began as a traffic light for your coding agents, in the MacBook notch. 2.0 makes the notch
the front door and **Brain** the room behind it: your agents, tasks, timer, notes, clipboard, usage
and security in one window that can fill the screen, while the notch still shows them at a glance.

Requires macOS 14.6 or later (unchanged since 1.2.0). Existing installs update in place.

---

### Brain

Settings is now **Brain**, and it leads with what Kannu watches, not with switches. Open it from
the brain icon in the notch, from the eye's menu in the menu bar, or with **Brain…** (⌘,).

- **Seven product pages:** Agents, Security, Usage, Tasks, Timer, Notes and Clipboard, in that
  order. Each opens on its live state, with its settings below; the gear in its header jumps there.
  - **Agents** leads with the chat that needs you most, then your recent chats, and offers Kannu's
    hook to any editor on this Mac that lacks it.
  - **Security** leads with the findings to review, with **Scan now**.
  - **Usage** shows each provider's limits, when each resets and when it was read, and the models
    you ran this week.
  - **Timer** leads with the running timer, and your presets as tiles that start one.
  - **Notes** lists your notes, pinned first, with **New note**.
  - **Clipboard** leads with your pinned items; History stays folded until you open it.
- **Settings, one row away.** Click **Settings** and the sidebar swaps to General, Appearance and
  the rest, with a Back row. Search finds any setting from any page.
- **Paper and ink.** Brain draws on paper in light and ink in dark, set in Geist, with the eye's
  blue as its one accent. Its appearance is its own: it can follow macOS, or stay light or dark
  while everything else follows the system.
- **Full screen.** **View › Enter Full Screen** (⌃⌘F) gives Brain the whole display, and ⌘W
  leaves full screen before Brain closes, so no empty Space is left behind. On a wide window, Tasks
  and Notes show what you open beside the list.
- **A menu bar of its own** while Brain is open: **Go** to any page (⌘1–⌘7); **View** to hide
  the sidebar (⌃⌘S), pick the appearance or go full screen; **File › New Task** (⌘N) from any
  page.
- **Badges only for what needs you:** a yellow dot on Agents while an agent waits for you, the count
  of high-severity findings on Security, a dot on Usage near a limit, and ▶ on Timer while one runs.
- **No cost out of sight.** Closed, minimised, covered or in another Space, Brain stops every live
  view, and it catches up when you come back.

### Tasks

**Brain › Tasks** puts your own tasks beside the ones assigned to you elsewhere, and the notch times
them. It stays off until you turn on **Enable tasks**.

- **Six sources, read-only.** Connect them under **Add a source**: Jira Cloud, GitLab (issues and
  merge requests), GitHub (github.com or your company's GitHub Enterprise Server), Linear, Todoist
  and Apple Reminders. Kannu never changes a ticket. A source syncs when you open Tasks, press
  Refresh or connect it, never on a timer, and tokens stay in your Mac's Keychain, in items only
  Kannu can read.
- **One list.** Active, Done and Archived tabs, a Find field and a Tag filter. On a wide window or in
  full screen, the task you open shows beside the list.
- **New task** (⌘N) takes a title, an estimate you type ("45", "90m", "1.5h", "2d 3h", where a day
  is an 8-hour workday), tags and a reminder. In the notch, a name typed in the timer tab becomes a
  task.
- **✓ and ▶ on the rows.** The tick marks one of your own tasks done, with a moment to take it
  back, and ▶ times any task. A synced task has no tick: its status belongs to its source.
- **The notch's Tasks button** shows what you are timing, what waits to be logged and what is next.
  The timer tab lists your tasks beside the presets.
- **Time to log asks first.** Time on a Jira or GitLab task waits as a draft, rounded to the nearest
  15 minutes, and goes to the server only when you click **Log**. It is the only thing Kannu ever
  sends to a source.
- **Closed tasks tidy themselves.** A done or closed task moves to the archive after 3 days, and an
  archived Jira or GitLab ticket clears 7 days later. Never while it holds time you have not logged,
  never while its server still lists it as open, and never your own tasks.

### Tags

- **Type them.** In a task's name, `#launch` becomes a tag and `@3pm` a reminder:
  `Ship notes #launch @3pm` saves as "Ship notes", tagged launch, with a reminder at the next 3 pm.
  `@noon`, `@morning`, `@evening`, `@tonight`, `@today`, `@tomorrow`, a weekday such as `@mon`, and
  times such as `@5:30pm` or `@17:30` all work, and a day and a time combine. They show as chips
  while you type. An issue number such as `#4264`, or an email address, stays in the title as typed.
- **Colours.** Each tag has one, and a task takes its first tag's colour, or its source's when it
  has no tags. Jira and GitLab labels count as tags.
- **`#tag` search finds the whole tag.** `#work` finds the tasks tagged work, not "Homework", and
  `#4264` still finds an issue key. It works in Brain's Find and in the notch's search.
- **One Tag filter,** in Brain and in the notch's filter menu. In the notch, adding a task that the
  filter would hide clears the filter, so the new task shows.
- **In the notch,** a task row shows its first tag when it fits and "+N" for the rest, and its time
  and any overtime always show whole.

### The notch

- **Hide it like closing an eye,** from the notch's own header button or its right-click menu. Click
  the eye in the menu bar and choose **Show Notch** to open it again.
- **It stays put when you switch Spaces,** and it stays open while your pointer is on it, menus and
  popovers included.
- **A running timer keeps the closed notch,** with the agent light in its centre, below the notch on
  a MacBook. On a monitor without a notch, the notch stays out while a timer runs.
- **Your colours.** Text and icons follow a light **Notch fill color** or skin, so they stay
  readable. **Horns**, a red and black skin, ships beside Default, and **Copy AI prompt** gives an
  image AI a prompt that designs a skin sized for the notch.
- **Geist in the notch too,** and the clipboard panel and the LocalSend picker take Brain's look.
- **Fullscreen video hides the notch again** under "Hide only when NowPlaying app is in fullscreen",
  including a video you made fullscreen before pressing play.

### Agents and usage

- **A steadier light.** A green chat no longer pins the notch out, and a momentary blip never
  flickers the light. A finished chat's red comes about a second late, on purpose. A chat stays
  green while the agents it started are still running, and Kannu stays light with several at once.
- **Hooks that follow each agent's rules.** Cursor chats no longer show up as Claude Code sessions,
  and Kannu answers Cursor's, Claude Code's, Codex's and Antigravity's hooks the way each one
  documents. Kannu upgrades its hook script itself.
- **Claude usage you can trust.** The card's Refresh fetches again, and signs Claude Code in when it
  has to. When Claude Code and Claude Desktop use different organizations, each gets a card. The
  limit bars follow the right organization and expire on time, and token totals leave out cache
  re-reads, which show apart.
- **Codex, Cursor and Antigravity.** Codex's limits come from Codex itself, Cursor splits its Auto
  and API pools, and Antigravity shows its model quotas when its CLI reports them.
- **Lighter on memory.** Reading token usage no longer climbs past a gigabyte: transcripts and
  session logs are read once, then only what was appended.

### A calmer first launch

A new install meets onboarding in Brain's look, with a **Permissions** step of its own. Each
permission says why Kannu wants it, and macOS asks only when you press **Allow**, one dialog at a
time. **Not now** keeps that feature off, with nothing asking later, until you allow it in
**Brain › Settings › General › Permissions**. The welcome music waits for **Finish**.

---

### Privacy

- **Prompts never touch the disk.** Kannu's agent hook used to copy each payload to a temporary
  file, and a hook killed at the wrong moment could leave one behind. It now reads the payload in
  memory, and Kannu deletes old leftovers at launch.
- **Clipboard history skips** copies their app marks private, and copied access tokens.
- **Codex's login token stays unread** for limits: they come from `codex app-server` or Codex's
  newest session log.
- **Saved secrets** get a fresh Keychain item that only Kannu can read.
- **Kannu's extension server listens on this Mac only,** not on your network.
- **Less access.** The microphone is asked for the first time you press Record in Screen Assistant,
  and the app no longer carries camera or Desktop folder access it never used.
- **ADR** install lines pin one upstream commit.

### What changes when you update

- **Settings is now Brain**, everywhere you see it. Product pages come first and the settings sit
  behind the Settings row, so a setting's path reads like **Brain › Settings › General**.
- **A short welcome.** The first launch after updating shows what is new in 2.0, once.
- **The minimal UI mode is gone,** and every notch is the standard one. If you used it,
  **Show media controls in Dynamic Island** is turned on for you, so the closed notch keeps your
  music. The minimal traffic-light style is a separate setting and stays.
- **Hovering opens the notch after 0.3 s** instead of 1 s. A value you set yourself is kept.
- **Apple Notes sync keeps your whole account** if you already sync. A note made in Notes now opens
  read-only in the notch, with **Open in Notes**, because editing it in Kannu lost its formatting. A
  new sync reads only a Kannu folder unless you choose **All my notes**.
- **Kannu's agent hook script upgrades itself** on first launch; your own hooks are left alone.
- **Reminders and Notifications** are asked for only when you first use them.
- **A new icon:** one eye, and an install window to match.
- **Kannu lives at [YANC-Kannu/kannu-macos](https://github.com/YANC-Kannu/kannu-macos).** Updates,
  releases and the crash and freeze reports you choose to send all point there.
- **Some new text is still in English** in other languages, until the translations catch up.

---

### Also fixed

- **The headphones animation plays.** Every earlier release shipped the Bluetooth HUD's clips as
  empty placeholders, so it drew an empty frame when your headphones connected.
- **Pinned clipboard images keep their files.** A relaunch used to delete them.
- **The open notch no longer closes under a still pointer** when a menu, popover or alert opens.
- A stopped timer no longer ends the next session, the alarm stops when a new one starts, and the
  timer's hours, minutes and seconds always show whole.
- A window rebuild no longer leaves the notch unable to close or scroll.
- Every icon-only control in the notch names itself in a tooltip, and the header's fixed buttons
  keep their place.
- Scrolling notes never closes or blurs the notch, opening a note no longer moves it to the top, and
  a saved note can be deleted from inside it.
- Brain's search puts first the rows whose title says what you typed, and reads "colour" and
  "color" alike.
