## Kannu 2.0.0 beta 2 — Horus

**This is a beta, for testers.** It is signed and notarized like a release, but Kannu's automatic
updates never offer it: you get it only by downloading the DMG below. When 2.0.0 ships, your Kannu
updates itself to it as usual.

Requires macOS 14.6 or later. It installs over 2.0.0 beta 1, 1.3.4 or a 1.4.0 beta and keeps your
settings.

### New since beta 1

- **The agent light sits beside the notch.** On a MacBook with a notch, each change shows the light
  to the left of the notch and the agent's app icon to the right, for a few seconds, then hands the
  notch back. Nothing stacks: the light goes ahead of music and a running timer, which come back
  with no light in them. The lock-screen pill shows the light and the icon too.
- **One light, a smaller icon.** The light is one lit dot, and the app icon beside it is a little
  smaller, 16 pt on a 14-inch MacBook Pro's notch.
- **Onboarding shows the light** instead of asking about it: its light step draws the real pill,
  working, waiting and finished.
- **A calmer welcome.** After onboarding, Kannu's eye opens in the notch and says "Welcome to Kannu",
  with a soft chime, once. With Reduce Motion it only fades, and with interface sounds off it is
  silent.
- **An alert when an agent needs you.** When an agent turns yellow, Kannu plays a sound and runs one
  shimmer across the notch, on the lock screen too. Each wait alerts once, and agents that turn
  yellow together alert once. The sound is None, Kannu alarm or a macOS sound, Glass unless you pick
  another. It stays off until you turn on Brain › Agents › Attention alert › Alert when an agent
  needs you.
- **Wispr Flow's meeting summaries in the notch's Notes.** Summaries from Wispr Flow's Notetaker
  list after your notes, read-only, with Copy and **Open in Wispr Flow**. Kannu reads them on this
  Mac and never changes them. They stay off until you turn on Brain › Notes › Wispr Flow › Show
  Wispr Flow meeting summaries, a section that shows when Wispr Flow is installed.
- **A calmer Notes in the notch.** "+" is the one filled button, and the rest of the header is plain
  glyphs, with no red or yellow. A card shows its note's first line instead of "Untitled Note",
  never repeats its title, and carries one colour, on its bar.
- **Tags on notes.** Add them in a note's tag row, or type `#launch` in its title. Notes and tasks
  share one set of tags, suggested wherever you type one, and a card shows its first tag and "+N".
  Tags stay in Kannu: they are never written into an Apple note.
- **One Filter menu in the notch's Notes,** for source (Kannu, Apple Notes or Wispr Flow), tag and
  colour. It is remembered, and a "Filtered · Show all" line shows while it hides notes.

### Fixed since beta 1

- **Claude no longer waits on Kannu.** Kannu's Claude Code hooks used to hold up every prompt and
  every tool call for a moment. Now they run beside Claude, which waits for them only when Kannu
  may have to answer, or when Claude asks you something.
- **Claude in the desktop app's Code tab.** A chat that ends its turn with a question now shows
  yellow, and stays yellow until you answer, even after the desktop app closes the chat's process in
  the background. A long single step keeps its green light while Claude says it is busy.
- **Project names.** A Claude Desktop chat in a worktree is named after its repository, not the
  worktree's random folder, and a Cursor chat in `acme-api` reads acme-api, not api.
- **The Claude card after an early reset.** When Claude resets a weekly limit early, the card shows
  the fresh percentage instead of the old one, also after Claude Code moves to another organization.
- **Time to log fits at full screen.** On a 14-inch Mac in full screen, its card in Brain › Tasks
  no longer cuts off its own text.

### What changes when you update

- **The light moves beside the notch** for everyone, updaters included. A display without a notch
  keeps its light in the middle, as before.
- **The Classic light and the band below the notch are gone,** with their settings in Brain › Agents.
  If you used either, you now get the one lit dot beside the notch.
- **Tasks is on by default,** unless you turned it off yourself. Turning it on asks for nothing:
  Reminders asks only when you connect it.
- **Kannu no longer runs as Claude Code's status line** unless you ask. If an earlier Kannu took it
  over, launch hands back your own, or none. To keep Kannu's live limits in a terminal, turn on
  Brain › Agents › Editor hooks › Live Claude Code limits, which is off by default.
- **Kannu's agent hook upgrades itself again** on first launch; your own hooks are left alone.
- **The alert and Wispr Flow's summaries start off,** so nothing new sounds or appears until you
  turn it on.

### Please try

- **With an agent working,** on a MacBook with a notch: switch it to working, then waiting, then
  stopped. Each switch should show a dot on the left and a still app icon on the right, with no
  band below, and collapse after about 7 seconds.
- **With music playing, or a timer running:** a switch should show the agent light in their place,
  then the music or the timer should return with no light in its middle.
- **Locked,** with the lock-screen live activity on, then off: a switch, or an agent waiting for
  you, should show the dot and the icon on the lock-screen pill.
- **Brain › Agents** should offer no light style and no Advanced position, and its preview should
  show the one dot beside the notch.
- **A fresh install's onboarding:** the light step should show three still pills in one card, with
  no empty space under it, and Tasks should be on afterwards, with no dialog.
- **Done at the end of onboarding:** the window fades, the eye opens in the notch, "Welcome to
  Kannu" shows for a moment, the notch closes, and one soft chime plays. Try it again with System
  Settings › Sound › "Play user interface sound effects" off (no chime) and with Reduce Motion on
  (fades only).
- **Reduce Motion** (System Settings › Accessibility › Display): the light should fade in and out,
  not slide.
- **The attention alert:** turn it on and press **Play sound**. Then let an agent ask you something,
  once with the Mac unlocked and once locked: one sound and one shimmer each time, and no second
  alert while it keeps waiting.
- **Claude Desktop's Code tab:** end a turn with a question. The light should turn yellow and stay
  yellow, minutes later too, until you answer.
- **Claude Code in a terminal:** prompts and tool calls should not pause for Kannu. With Live Claude
  Code limits off, your own status line, or none, should be back.
- **Wispr Flow,** if you have it: turn the summaries on and open the notch's Notes. Your meetings
  should list after your notes and open read-only.
- **Notes in the notch:** type `Standup #team` as a new note's title, then filter by that tag, then
  by source, and press **Show all**.
- **Project names:** a Claude Desktop worktree chat should show its repository's name on its card.

### Going back

Please stay on the beta until 2.0.0 ships. It upgrades Kannu's agent hook and changes some stored
settings, and going back to 1.3.4 or a 1.4.0 beta over them has not been tested. If you have to,
tell David first.
