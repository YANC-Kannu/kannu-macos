## Kannu 2.0.0 beta 2 — Horus

**This is a beta, for testers.** It is signed and notarized like a release, but Kannu's automatic
updates never offer it: you get it only by downloading the DMG below. When 2.0.0 ships, your Kannu
updates itself to it as usual.

Requires macOS 14.6 or later. It installs over 2.0.0 beta 1, 1.3.4 or a 1.4.0 beta and keeps your
settings.

### New since beta 1

- **The agent light sits beside the notch.** On a MacBook with a notch, each change shows the light
  to the left of the notch and the agent's app icon to the right, for a few seconds, then hands the
  notch back. Nothing stacks: the light goes ahead of music and a running timer, which come back
  with no light in them. The lock-screen pill shows the light and the icon too.
- **The band below the notch is still there,** for everyone who liked it: Brain › Agents › Traffic
  light › Advanced › Agent light position › Below the notch.

### What changes when you update

- **The light moves beside the notch** for everyone, updaters included. A display without a notch
  keeps its light in the middle, as before.

### Please try

- **With an agent working,** on a MacBook with a notch: switch it to working, then waiting, then
  stopped. Each switch should show a dot on the left and a still app icon on the right, with no
  band below, and collapse after about 7 seconds.
- **With music playing, or a timer running:** a switch should show the agent light in their place,
  then the music or the timer should return with no light in its middle.
- **Locked,** with the lock-screen live activity on, then off: a switch, or an agent waiting for
  you, should show the dot and the icon on the lock-screen pill.
- **Brain › Agents › Traffic light › Advanced › Below the notch** should bring back the band
  exactly as beta 1 drew it.
- **Reduce Motion** (System Settings › Accessibility › Display): the light should fade in and out,
  not slide.

## Kannu 2.0.0 beta 2 — Horus

### Fixed since beta 1

- **Claude in the desktop app's Code tab.** A chat that ends its turn with a question now shows
  yellow, and stays yellow until you answer, even after the desktop app closes the chat's process in
  the background. A long single step keeps its green light while Claude says it is busy.
